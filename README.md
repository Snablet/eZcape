## 📖 Overview

**eZcape** is a top-down RPG roguelike with survival horror elements. Set within the twisted corridors of a hospital-like laboratory, you must navigate floor by floor, upgrade your abilities, and make choices that determine your fate. Your brother—a brilliant but morally broken scientist—has injected you with a prototype zombie virus as part of a forbidden life experiment. Now, the facility has collapsed into chaos. Every scientist has turned. Your mind is fading. And the clock is ticking.

---

## 🧬 Story Synopsis

> *"He told me it was for science. For progress. For us."*

Your brother has spent decades researching human biology and neurobiology. His obsession with unlocking the boundaries of consciousness led him to create something he could not control—a prototype zombie virus designed to rewrite the human mind. You were never meant to be a test subject. But in his pursuit of discovery, family became expendable.

You wake up disoriented, feverish, and alone. The facility is overrun. The scientists are no longer human. And somewhere deep inside you, the virus is taking hold. Your only hope lies in finding your brother. His office—located on the lowest floor—holds the cure. But the deeper you go, the more you lose yourself.

Will you find the cure and confront the monster who did this to you? Or will you abandon the search and escape the facility, leaving behind answers—and your humanity?

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Mechanics

### 🏢 Floor-Based Progression

- The game is structured across **multiple floors**, starting from the top level and descending toward your brother's office on the bottom floor.
- Each floor is procedurally generated, offering unique layouts, enemy placements, and loot opportunities.
- **Timed Floors:** You have a limited amount of time to complete each floor. When the timer hits zero, zombies flood the area and capture you—resulting in a run-ending encounter.
- Timer resets at the start of every new floor.

---

### 💉 The Consciousness System

The virus doesn't just affect your body—it affects your mind. As time passes, your consciousness deteriorates.

- **Syringes:** Scattered throughout the facility, these injectable items restore **4 minutes** to the floor timer.
- **Effects of Losing Consciousness:**
  - Visual distortion (motion blur, screen wobble, vignette darkening)
  - Auditory decay (sounds become muffled, distorted, or glitched)
  - The protagonist's inner voice becomes fragmented, echoing, or corrupted
- The longer you go without a syringe, the more the game world becomes unstable.

---

### 🎙️ Inner Voice System

The protagonist has a constant inner monologue that reflects their mental state. As consciousness fades, so does the clarity of their voice.

- **Normal:** Clear, coherent thoughts. Commentary on the environment, items, and enemies.
- **Corrupt:** Words begin to slur. Sentences break. Paranoia creeps in.
- **Rotten:** Incoherent whispers, fragmented memories, and distorted audio that bleeds into the soundtrack.

You can also choose between three **voice effect presets** in the audio settings to customize how the decay sounds.

---

### ⚔️ Character Progression

As you descend, you'll earn experience and find upgrade modules to improve core attributes:

| Attribute | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Strength** | Increases melee damage and carry weight |
| **Stamina** | Affects sprint duration and dodge efficiency |
| **Endurance** | Boosts health pool and resistance to status effects |
| **Perception** | Improves critical hit chance and reveals enemy weak points |
| **Agility** | Increases movement speed and attack speed |

---

### 🛡️ Loot & Equipment

Explore each floor to find:

- **Weapons** – From scalpels and IV stands to makeshift axes and lab-grade experimental firearms.
- **Armor** – Lab coats (minimal protection), tactical vests, reinforced riot gear.
- **Consumables** – Bandages, adrenaline shots, and of course—syringes.
- **Key Items** – Passcodes, keycards, and research notes that unlock shortcuts and reveal lore.

## 👾 Bosses & Enemy Factions

Each major floor is guarded by a unique boss, each representing a department within the corrupted facility.

### Mini-Bosses (Encountered Throughout)

| Boss | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🧪 **The Chemist** | A toxicologist consumed by his own creations. Hurls chemicals, leaves poison pools, and weakens you over time. |
| 🔪 **The Surgeon** | Precise, fast, and deadly. Low health but high damage—demands patience and timing. |
| ⚰️ **The Embalmer** | A hulking morgue worker with grotesque durability. Slow but relentless. High health, low damage output. Appears around 75% completion. |
| 🩺 **The Nurse** | Summons zombified patients to overwhelm you. Can heal herself if left unchecked. High priority target. |

---

### Final Bosses (Ending-Dependent)

Your choice near the end determines which final confrontation awaits you.

## 🚪 The Choice – Two Endings

At **70–75% game completion**, after defeating The Embalmer, you are presented with a critical decision:

### 🧪 Path 1: The Cure Ending

*You choose to find the cure.*

- You must defeat **The Nurse** to obtain the key to your brother's office.
- Enter the office. Confront **The Brother**.
- The battle tests everything you've learned—a duel of flesh, will, and twisted family loyalty.
- **Ending:** You find the cure. But what's left of you after everything?

> *"You hold the syringe in trembling hands. It was always his. But now it's yours."*

---

### 🚪 Path 2: The Escape Ending

*You choose to leave it all behind.*

- Instead of pursuing the cure, you search for an exit from the facility.
- Your escape route leads you to the loading docks, where you're intercepted by **Uncle**, a police officer who arrived to investigate the facility—and who now sees you as a threat.
- **Ending:** You escape into the outside world. But the virus is still inside you. And so is everything you left unanswered.

> *"The air tastes like rain. Freedom. You look back once. The building watches. Silent. Waiting."*

## 🎵 Audio & Sound Design

As the **Sound Engineer**, I've designed the audio to reflect the game's psychological horror and tonal duality.

- **Adaptive Soundtrack:** Music shifts dynamically based on player status—calm exploration, elevated tension, boss encounters, and consciousness decay all trigger layered changes.
- **Inner Voice System:** Three distinct vocal states with custom effects and performance styles.
- **Sound Effects:** A curated selection of realistic, corrupted, and "rotten" audio presets, letting players tailor their auditory experience.
- **Zombie Vocals:** Recorded and processed team voice work—no generic roars. These zombies sound confused, pained, and disturbingly human.
